Why We're Needed

Evidence-based modelling for 100-year coastal planning

New national regulations require councils across New Zealand to map coastal risk 100 years ahead while safeguarding marine ecosystems. SEA-Mod provides the rigorous modelling evidence needed to achieve both.

We specialized in sub-contracting support for premier consultancies and direct tenders for Regional, District, City, and Unitary Councils along the Central-West Coast of the North Island.

Policy & Regulatory Framework Alignment

  • NZCPS: New Zealand Coastal Policy Statement compliance.
  • NPS-NH: National Policy Statement - Natural Hazards risk mapping.
  • EIANZ 2018: Environment Institute guidelines & Marine Addendum.
  • Privacy Act 2020: Bank-grade AES-256 / TLS 1.3 NZ cloud storage & data sovereignty.

Cultural Responsibility

A Simple Process for Every Project

We recognize the distinct position of Māori as Tangata Whenua. We seek Mana Whenua and community input early before shaping adaptation options.

01
Before Starting: Identify & agree on relevant stakeholders, map engagement, and establish clear data-use expectations.
02
During Analysis: Listen & adapt. Invite relevant perspectives, check assumptions, and safeguard sensitive information.
03
At Delivery: Explain & account. Communicate uncertainty clearly and respect agreed data-sharing limits.

Our Cultural Principles

  • Respectful Engagement: Early consultation before shaping technical options.
  • Inclusive Practice: Build diverse teams and challenge assumptions.
  • Responsible Data Use: Safeguard sensitive cultural knowledge and respect data sovereignty.
